# Island Deathmatch — architect notes (macro layout)

Generated by `/workspace/gen_world.py` (deterministic, seeded per cell) → `/workspace/world.json`.
Re-run `python3 gen_world.py` to regenerate; it self-checks cell count (225), prop/scatter budgets,
structure footprint overlaps, and road-strip clearance before writing.

## Sizing & zoning
- 15×15 cells at `cell_size:16` → 240 m square, cells `[-7,-7]..[7,7]`, all 225 present & contiguous.
  Sized for 16 players (~60 m mean spacing per the multiplayer sizing rule).
- Zones by Chebyshev ring r = max(|gx|,|gz|):
  - **r ≤ 2 — URBAN CORE** (5×5): road grid (ns on gx∈{-2,0,2}, ew on gz∈{-2,0,2}, `x` crossroads,
    width 7), 124 parametric structures total (concrete/glass, `facade:"windows"`, floors 4–20,
    varied footprint/profile/cap/rot — profiles taper/setback appear on 13+ floor towers). Ground
    `asphalt` on road cells, `concrete` on the 4 interior block cells. Street dressing: crates,
    barrels, planters, street trees, kept off road strips.
  - **r = 3 — OUTSKIRTS** (transition): farm cells that also carry 1–2 small concrete structures
    (2–4 floors, flat/gable) on ~half the cells, so city → farm flows.
  - **r = 4 — FARMLAND**: barns/silos/windmills/water towers from `props/q_farmbuild/` + fenced
    field rectangles (Fence rows) + crop/bush/tree scatter, ground grass/dirt.
  - **r = 5 — FARM→HILL transition**: sparse meadows, fence remnants, occasional SmallBarn, more trees.
  - **r = 6–7 — HILLS** (island edges): rocky/grassy wilderness, tree/rock/bush scatter + a few
    placed rocks/stumps/logs; ground weighted toward `rock` on the outermost ring.
- **Terrain**: `{amplitude:7, frequency:0.012, seed:1177, octaves:4, material:"grass"}` — rolling
  hills; city/farm cells override ground per-cell, roads drape the terrain.

  1 MultiMesh draw each.
